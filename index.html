What we do

Built right the first time, then kept that way

Fences and decks built to last, with attention to detail and high-quality materials. Lawn and property maintenance, from mowing to fertilizing, that keeps a place looking its best year-round. Commercial and residential.

Fences & gates

Privacy fence, ranch rail, pipe and custom gates. Set square, built plumb, and finished to last in Texas weather. New builds, replacements and repairs.

Decks & patios

Decks, patios and outdoor living space framed and finished to the same standard as the house they attach to. Built for shade, weather and the way you actually use the yard.

Lawn care & sod

Mowing, edging and fertilizing on a schedule that keeps a yard ahead of the season, plus fresh Bermuda sod when a lawn needs starting over instead of saving.

Tree work & stump grinding

Trimming, pruning, removal and stump grinding. Done safely, with the site cleaned up before we leave.

Beds, mulch & landscaping

Flower bed cleanup, fresh mulch, and custom bed and landscape design suited to the property, and to the heat it has to survive.

Pressure washing & gutters

Driveways, walkways, siding and fences brought back to clean, and gutters cleared out before the season turns.

Property clearing & cleanup

Brush, debris and overgrowth hauled out to get a place back to usable, whether that's a back yard or a few acres.

Questions

Before you call

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Cedar Creek and work throughout Bastrop County: Bastrop, Elgin, Smithville, Paige, Red Rock, Rosanky, McDade, Wyldwood, Dale, and out toward Del Valle and Mustang Ridge. If you're just outside that, call and we'll tell you straight whether we can get to you.

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. Call or text (512) 575-5452, describe the property, and we'll set a time to walk it and put a number to the work.

What kinds of fences do you build?

Privacy fence, ranch rail, pipe fence and custom gates, in wood or metal depending on what the property calls for. We do new builds, full replacements, and repairs to existing fence lines.

Do you work on commercial property as well as homes?

Both. The same crew and the same standard, whether it's a back yard in Cedar Creek or a commercial lot that needs keeping up on a schedule.

Can I hire you for a one-time job, or do I have to sign up for regular service?

Either. Plenty of our work is a single fence, deck or cleanup. If you want the property kept up afterward, we can put you on a regular schedule, but there's no obligation to.

How soon can you start?

It depends on the season and the size of the job. Call and we'll give you an honest window rather than an optimistic one.
