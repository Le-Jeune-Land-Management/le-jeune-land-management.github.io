What we do

We build it right the first time!

We provide attention to details, construction techniques and materials utilized to ensure improved strength, durability and longevity of your landscape project. We want our clients to be pleasantly surprised when we are finished and walking away from your property. And if all you need us to provide is basic lawn care, we will still serve you with integrity and excellence.

Services we offer

Lawn & property maintenance

Scheduled weekly or bi-weekly lawn and property maintenance service to include mowing, edging and blowing off clippings.

Lawn improvements

Site prep and installation of new sod, lawn reseeding, lawn fertilization and aeration services.

Tree trimming & stump grinding

Trim trees for healthy growth and improved appearance. Grind down stumps of removed or fallen trees to improve mowing ability and property appearance.

Pressure washing

Pressure wash to thoroughly clean and improve the appearance of sidewalks, driveways, parking areas, brick and stone walls, etc.

Seasonal property refresh

Clear out old vegetation growth, refresh flower beds, install fresh mulch as needed and prepare your property for the next season.

Land & property clearing

Clear out and remove underbrush, overgrowth, unwanted trees, etc. to improve the usability and appearance of your property.

Fence design & construction

Design and build the right fence for your privacy and property needs.

Deck design & construction

Design and build decks, pergolas and gazebos for improving your property's enjoyment and aesthetic.

Landscape design & construction

Design and implement or refresh your property landscaping elements. Construction of new elements such as flower beds, etc. or redesign/refresh existing elements.

Raised-bed garden design & construction

Design and build your dream raised-bed garden for growing your own fruits and vegetables.

Urban and country homestead design & implementation

Aesthetic design & implementation of your homestead dreams. Plan your property utilization and where each homestead element should be located. Establish phases of property development for a clear vision forward. Build and establish each homestead element in your determined order and in your predetermined property locale.

Need something that isn't on this list? Just call and ask us! If it's an outdoor project, we can likely help.

Questions

Do you charge for estimates?

No. Estimates are free. Call or text (512) 575-5452, describe the property, and we'll set a time to walk it and put a number to the work.

What kind of fences do you build?

Privacy in various materials, wood or metal construction techniques, traditional or craftsman styles. Chain link and ranch rail. We offer new builds, full replacements and repairs to existing fences.

Do you offer commercial services as well as residential?

We serve both! The same crew with the same high-quality standard, whether it's in a Bastrop back yard or a commercial property that needs regularly scheduled maintenance.

Can I hire you for a one-time job, or do I have to sign up for regular service?

We offer both. Plenty of our projects are one-time jobs such as fences, decks or seasonal property cleanup. But we also offer regularly scheduled maintenance services as well. We will provide whatever type of service you are looking for. If you want to be added to our schedule, give us a call.

How soon can you start?

This will depend on the season and the size of your project. Give us a call and we will give you a specific service day if you are needing lawn maintenance. If you are needing a larger outdoor construction project, we will schedule a meeting for a quote and then add you to our project schedule at the earliest opening we have.
